# Claims Process Manual — All Lines (Extract)

Document CLM-PRC-2026. Governs the handling of motor and health claims for all
2026 policies. Clauses are numbered P-x.y for precise citation in customer
communication and audits.

## Section P-1 — Registration and intimation

P-1.1 Intimation channels: Claims may be intimated by phone, portal or branch,
24 hours a day. The intimation records the policy number, the event date and a
brief description.

P-1.2 Claim registration: Every intimation is registered with a unique claim
identifier in the format CLM-XXXX, quoted in all subsequent communication.

P-1.3 Acknowledgement: An acknowledgement stating the claim identifier and the
document checklist is issued within 24 hours of intimation.

P-1.4 Surveyor timelines: For motor own-damage claims, a licensed surveyor is
appointed within 48 hours of intimation. The surveyor inspects the vehicle and
submits the survey report within 15 days of inspection. A motor own-damage
claim cannot be approved before the survey report has been received; the claim
remains Under Review with next step "Surveyor report awaited" until then.

## Section P-2 — Document verification

P-2.1 Verification: On registration the claim enters document verification and
carries the status Received. Each checklist item is verified against the
policy requirements (for health claims, see H-5.1 to H-5.3).

P-2.2 Deficiency letters: Where documents are missing or illegible, a
deficiency letter listing the outstanding items is issued within 7 days; the
claim does not progress until the items are received.

P-2.3 Referral: Indicators of misrepresentation are referred to the
investigation cell; the customer is informed that verification is extended.

## Section P-3 — Review

P-3.1 Status definitions: A claim moves through the statuses Received
(documents under verification), Under Review (assessment in progress),
Approved (payable amount determined), Rejected (not payable, clauses cited)
and Paid (settlement transferred and claim closed).

P-3.2 Review timeline: A decision is issued within 30 days of receipt of the
complete document set, including any survey report or medical officer opinion.

P-3.3 Specialist referral: Motor claims requiring re-inspection are returned
to the surveyor; health claims with possible pre-existing conditions are
referred to the medical officer under H-7.1.

## Section P-4 — Approval and settlement

P-4.1 Payment: Approved amounts are transferred electronically within 3 to 5
working days of the approval letter, consistent with H-6.1.

P-4.2 Settlement statement: The approval letter itemises the claimed amount,
each deduction with the clause applied (for example depreciation under M-5.1
or room-rent capping under H-4.2), and the approved amount.

## Section P-5 — Rejection, appeal and escalation

P-5.1 Rejection communication: A rejection letter states every ground of
rejection with the specific policy clause relied upon (for example licence
validity under M-2.3).

P-5.2 Appeal: The customer may appeal in writing within 30 days of the date of
the decision letter. The letter states the appeal window closing date. Appeals
are decided by an officer not involved in the original decision within 15 days
of receipt.

P-5.3 Escalation: If the appeal outcome is not acceptable, the customer may
approach the grievance redressal officer and thereafter the Insurance
Ombudsman; contact details are provided in the rejection and appeal letters.
