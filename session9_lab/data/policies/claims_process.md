# Claims Process Guide — All Products

Document code: PROCESS-STD-2026. All clauses are numbered for precise reference
in claims correspondence and customer communication.

## Section P-1 — Registration and Verification

P-1.1 Intimation: Claims may be intimated by phone, app or branch. Motor
accidents must be intimated within 48 hours (M-6.1); health hospitalisation
within 48 hours of admission for cashless, or at discharge for reimbursement.

P-1.2 Registration: On intimation, a claim ID in the format CLM-XXXX is issued
and an acknowledgement is sent by SMS and email. The claim enters status
"Received".

P-1.3 Document verification: Submitted documents are verified within 3 working
days of receipt. If any mandatory document is missing (for example a discharge
summary, H-8.1), the claim remains in "Received" and the processing clock is
paused until the document arrives.

P-1.4 Surveyor timelines: For motor claims, a licensed surveyor is appointed
within 48 hours of registration. The surveyor inspects the vehicle and submits
the report within 15 days of appointment. The claim remains "Under Review"
until the surveyor report is received and assessed.

## Section P-2 — Review and Settlement

P-2.1 Status meanings: "Received" — registered, documents under verification.
"Under Review" — assessment in progress (surveyor or medical officer).
"Approved" — amount sanctioned, payment initiated. "Paid" — settlement
transferred and claim closed. "Rejected" — claim declined with a clause
reference and appeal information.

P-2.2 Approval and payment: Once approved, payment is transferred by NEFT
within 3-5 working days. The settlement letter itemises every deduction with
its clause reference (for example depreciation under M-5.1, or a room-rent cap
adjustment under H-4.2).

P-2.3 Medical officer review: Health claims flagged for pre-existing condition
checks (H-7.1) are decided within 7 working days of receiving complete past
treatment records.

## Section P-3 — Rejection and Appeal

P-3.1 Rejection and appeal window: Every rejection letter states the exact
policy clause relied upon (for example M-2.3 for licence validity). The
policyholder may appeal in writing within 30 days of the rejection letter,
attaching any new documentary evidence. Appeals are decided within 15 working
days.

P-3.2 Escalation: If the appeal outcome is unsatisfactory, the case may be
escalated to the Grievance Redressal Officer, who responds within 10 working
days.

## Section P-4 — External Remedies

P-4.1 Ombudsman: After the internal process, complaints may be filed with the
Insurance Ombudsman within one year. Filing is free of charge and does not
require a lawyer.

P-4.2 Data requests: Policyholders may request a copy of the surveyor report
or medical officer opinion relied upon in the decision; it is provided within
7 working days.
