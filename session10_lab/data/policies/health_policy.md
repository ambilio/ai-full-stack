# Health Insurance Policy — Standard Terms (Individual & Family Floater)

Document code: HEALTH-STD-2026. All clauses are numbered for precise reference
in claims correspondence and customer communication.

## Section H-1 — Coverage

H-1.1 Scope of cover: This policy covers reasonable and customary charges for
in-patient hospitalisation of 24 hours or more, day-care procedures on the
published list, and ambulance charges up to the schedule limit.

H-1.2 Sum insured: All admissible claims in a policy year are payable up to the
sum insured stated on the schedule, inclusive of the sub-limits in Section H-4.

## Section H-2 — Waiting Periods

H-2.1 Initial waiting period: No claim other than for accidental injury is
payable within the first 30 days of the first policy start date.

H-2.2 Specified conditions: Named conditions (cataract, hernia, joint
replacement and others listed in the schedule annexure) carry a 24-month
waiting period.

## Section H-3 — Cashless and Reimbursement

H-3.1 Cashless facility: Available at network hospitals with pre-authorisation;
the hospital submits the request and the insurer responds within 2 working
hours during business days.

H-3.2 Reimbursement: For non-network hospitals, submit the claim with original
bills within 15 days of discharge.

## Section H-4 — Sub-limits and Capping

H-4.1 Sub-limits: Specific procedures carry payment caps listed in the
schedule annexure; amounts above a cap are borne by the policyholder.

H-4.2 Room rent capping: Daily room rent is covered up to 1% of the sum
insured (2% for ICU). If a higher room category is chosen, all associated
charges — room, surgeon and procedure charges billed by room category — are
proportionately reduced in the same ratio. The deduction is itemised in the
settlement letter as "room-rent cap adjustment".

## Section H-5 — Exclusions

H-5.1 General exclusions: Cosmetic treatment, unproven therapies, self-inflicted
injury, treatment for alcohol or substance abuse, and non-medical consumables
on the published exclusion list.

## Section H-6 — Pre and Post Hospitalisation

H-6.1 Cover window: Related medical expenses 30 days before admission and
60 days after discharge are payable under the same claim, subject to the sum
insured.

## Section H-7 — Pre-existing Conditions

H-7.1 Pre-existing disease review: Conditions diagnosed or treated within
36 months before the first policy start date are covered only after 36 months
of continuous cover. Claims where a pre-existing condition is suspected are
referred to a medical officer for review of past records; the review may
request treatment history before a decision is issued. Non-disclosure of a
known pre-existing condition at proposal can lead to rejection.

## Section H-8 — Claim Conditions

H-8.1 Required documents: Claim form, discharge summary, original itemised
bills, investigation reports, and prescriptions. The discharge summary is
mandatory; verification does not begin until it is received (see P-1.3 of the
Claims Process Guide).
