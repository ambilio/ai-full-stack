# Health Insurance Policy — Individual Hospitalisation Cover (Extract)

Document HLT-POL-2026. Applies to all individual hospitalisation policies
issued in 2026, including POL-11907, POL-73310 and POL-99120. Clauses are
numbered H-x.y for precise citation in claim decisions and appeals.

## Section H-1 — Cover

H-1.1 Scope of cover: This policy covers reasonable and customary charges for
in-patient hospitalisation of the insured person exceeding 24 hours, including
room rent, intensive care, medical practitioner fees, diagnostics, medicines
and listed day-care procedures.

H-1.2 Policy period and renewal: Cover operates for hospitalisation commencing
within the policy period. Continuous renewal preserves accrued waiting-period
credits.

## Section H-2 — Waiting periods

H-2.1 Initial waiting period: No claim is payable for illness whose symptoms
first occur within 30 days of the first policy commencement, except
hospitalisation due to accident.

H-2.2 Specified conditions: Named conditions listed in the schedule (including
cataract, hernia and joint replacement) carry a 24-month waiting period.

## Section H-3 — Modes of settlement

H-3.1 Cashless facility: At network hospitals, pre-authorised treatment is
settled directly with the hospital; pre-authorisation must be sought at least
48 hours before planned admission or within 24 hours of emergency admission.

H-3.2 Reimbursement: At non-network hospitals, the insured pays and claims
reimbursement; the complete document set must be submitted within 15 days of
discharge.

## Section H-4 — Limits and capping

H-4.1 Sum insured: The total of all claims in a policy year cannot exceed the
sum insured stated in the schedule.

H-4.2 Room rent capping: Room rent is capped at 1% of the sum insured per day
(2% per day for intensive care). Where the insured occupies a room of a higher
category than the entitled category, all associated charges — including
medical practitioner fees, surgery charges and hospital services billed by
room category — are proportionately reduced in the ratio of the entitled room
rent to the occupied room rent. The approved amount may therefore be lower
than the claimed amount even where treatment is fully admissible.

## Section H-5 — Claim documents

H-5.1 Discharge summary: The hospital discharge summary is a mandatory claim
document. A claim cannot progress beyond document verification until the
discharge summary, stating diagnosis, treatment given and period of admission,
has been received.

H-5.2 Supporting documents: Original hospital bills, payment receipts,
investigation reports and the claim form signed by the insured are required.

H-5.3 Deficiency: Where documents are incomplete, a deficiency letter is
issued and the claim remains in verification until the listed documents are
received.

## Section H-6 — Settlement

H-6.1 Payment timeline: Approved claims are paid by electronic transfer within
3 to 5 working days of the approval letter.

H-6.2 Deduction statement: The settlement letter itemises every deduction with
the clause applied, including room-rent capping under H-4.2.

## Section H-7 — Pre-existing conditions and disclosure

H-7.1 Pre-existing diseases: Conditions diagnosed, treated or showing signs or
symptoms before the first policy commencement are covered only after 36 months
of continuous cover. Where hospital records indicate a possible pre-existing
condition, the claim is referred to the insurer's medical officer for review
before a decision is issued; the claim status remains Under Review during this
check.

H-7.2 Disclosure: The insured must disclose all known medical conditions at
proposal and renewal. Non-disclosure of a material condition entitles the
insurer to reject related claims.
