# Motor Insurance Policy — Standard Terms (Private Vehicles)

Document code: MOTOR-STD-2026. All clauses are numbered for precise reference in
claims correspondence and customer communication.

## Section M-1 — Coverage

M-1.1 Scope of cover: This policy covers loss of or damage to the insured
vehicle arising from accident, fire, lightning, theft, riot, strike, flood and
transit by road or rail, subject to the exclusions in Section M-4.

M-1.2 Policy period: Cover is effective from the policy start date to the end
date printed on the schedule. Claims arising from incidents outside this period
are not payable under any circumstance.

M-1.3 Geographical limits: Cover applies to incidents occurring within India
only, unless a specific endorsement extends the geographical area.

## Section M-2 — Driver and Usage Conditions

M-2.1 Authorised drivers: The vehicle must be driven by the policyholder or a
named driver listed on the schedule at the time of the incident.

M-2.2 Permitted use: The vehicle must be used only for private, social and
commuting purposes. Use for hire, reward, racing or commercial delivery voids
cover for the incident concerned.

M-2.3 Licence validity: The driver must hold a licence that is valid and
effective on the date and time of the accident. A claim will be rejected in
full where the driver's licence had expired, was suspended, or was of the wrong
class for the vehicle on the accident date. An appeal may be filed only with
documentary proof of a licence renewal effective before the accident.

## Section M-3 — What Is Payable

M-3.1 Own damage: Repair or replacement costs at a garage, assessed by a
licensed surveyor, less depreciation (M-5.1) and the deductible on the schedule.

M-3.2 Third-party liability: Liability to third parties for injury or property
damage as required under the Motor Vehicles Act, handled through the designated
legal process.

M-3.3 Total loss: Where repair cost exceeds 75% of the insured declared value
(IDV), the claim is settled at IDV less deductible, and the salvage passes to
the insurer (M-5.2).

## Section M-4 — Exclusions

M-4.1 General exclusions: Wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown,
consequential loss, and damage to tyres and tubes unless the vehicle is damaged
in the same incident.

M-4.2 Impairment: No claim is payable where the driver was under the influence
of alcohol or intoxicating drugs at the time of the accident.

## Section M-5 — Deductions

M-5.1 Depreciation schedule: Approved amounts are reduced by depreciation on
replaced parts: rubber, nylon and plastic parts and batteries at 50%;
fibreglass at 30%; metal parts per the vehicle-age slab (5% for vehicles aged
6 months to 1 year, rising by slab to 50% beyond 10 years). Depreciation is
itemised in the settlement letter.

M-5.2 Salvage: Salvage value of replaced parts or a total-loss vehicle is
deducted from, or transferred as part of, the settlement.

## Section M-6 — Claim Conditions

M-6.1 Intimation window: Accidents must be intimated within 48 hours of
occurrence; theft within 24 hours, along with the police FIR.

M-6.2 Required documents: Claim form, registration certificate, driving
licence of the driver at the time of the accident, repair estimate, and FIR
where applicable. Missing documents pause the claim clock (see P-1.3 of the
Claims Process Guide).
